### C++ (huffman.cpp)  input: abracadabra 
Enter text to encode: 
Char	Freq	Code

'b'	2	10
'c'	1	1110
'd'	1	1111
'r'	2	110

Original size  : 88 bits

Space Complexity: O(n)
